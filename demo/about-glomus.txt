GLOMUS - Upward, not northward

It's a little living landscape seen from above, with rivers, lakes, meadows and snow on the peaks.
You click a spot and type a whim: "carve a stream from here into the lake", "make the stones here
sweat water", "a floodgate that bursts every 30 seconds", "let glowing moss creep along the riverbanks".

An AI running on my own PC (no cloud) makes it happen. It doesn't just paint the map; it writes new
rules into how the world works. Before committing, it tests its plan in a quick simulation, sees what
went wrong, and fixes it. You can watch it think in the sidebar.

Then everything you've asked for keeps running and starts tangling together. The moss follows the
water the floodgate releases, rain turns the peaks white, and the meltwater finds the new streams.
Nothing has to be useful. You just watch what your whims turn into.

The name is Latin for a ball of thread. Every whim adds threads, and the world is what they wind into.
Very early prototype, but it works.
